## 1. `the-distance` — DILEMMA · you, 19

- **Psych anchor:** trickle truth; Glass's walls & windows.
- **Door:** hint "The door in two cities at once" · teaser "Nothing
  happened. That's the sentence you keep rehearsing."
- **Premise:** you and Jules survived September (dynamic beat callbacks to
  `promised-september` / `first-open` / `let-it-drift` where present). Last
  night, at a party, Alena's mouth was an inch from yours for one full
  second before you stepped back. Nothing happened. You have rehearsed the
  sentence eleven times.
- **Beats (5):** the party replayed at forensic speed (Chattam: the second
  of it, dilated across a beat) → the step back — real, and barely → the
  call with Jules tonight, scheduled, ordinary → the rehearsal count →
  Porter: "Porter: Guests file 'almost' under either confession or
  inventory. The filing decides what the word becomes."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `confess-the-near-miss` — tell Jules exactly what almost happened. ·
    lucidity +20, Heart +6, Ours +6. Outcome: a hard, good call; Jules's
    hurt is real and so is the window it opens — "thank you for telling me
    while it was still nothing"; the relationship gains a load-bearing
    honesty (flag `windows-open`, read by `just-friends`).
  - `bury-it` — nothing happened; nothing to tell. · lucidity +6, Mine +4,
    flags `buried-the-almost`. Outcome: technically true; the wall goes up
    one brick high; `just-friends` reads this flag and starts one wall
    taller.
  - `soften-it` — tell a curated version ("someone flirted with me, it was
    weird"). · lucidity +8, flags `trickle-truth`. Outcome: the trickle-
    truth trap sprung honestly — the softened story *works*, and now the
    true story can never be told without revealing the soft one was
    curated; the room closes on that arithmetic.
  - `keep-visiting-almost` — decide "almost" is a place you can keep
    visiting. · lucidity +4, Grip −4, Heart +4, flags `visiting-almost`.
    Outcome: written from inside the rationalization at full strength — the
    fizz of it, the deniability; the room grants the pleasure and prices it:
    Act II's `just-friends` becomes near-unavoidable in the door pool
    (weighting note for implementation).
- **Explanation:** trickle truth (partial confessions that foreclose full
  ones); near-miss disclosure research; why "technically nothing happened"
  and "nothing to tell" are different sentences.
- **Field note:** *The Curated Confession* — **Shirley Glass** · body:
  disclosure architecture; the finding that betrayed partners consistently
  rate the *drip* of revisions as worse than the original act; walls built
  one deniable brick at a time. Margin: *"almost" is a suburb. People
  commute.*
- **Reflections:** yes. **Icon:** two city skylines, a hairline gap.
  **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** two phone-glow points at opposite ends of
  a long low shelf.

## 2. `the-hall-pass` — DILEMMA · you, 20

- **Psych anchor:** consent under social pressure; Conley & Moors (CNM —
  negotiated vs. conceded).
- **Door:** hint "The door that says it's a gift" · teaser "It's presented
  with a bow on it. Your stomach disagrees."
- **Premise:** Jules proposes exceptions-while-apart — framed as modern,
  generous, secure. You don't want it. Saying so feels clingy, jealous,
  young — every adjective you're afraid of. The room's craft: the pressure
  is *social register*, not malice; Jules means well, which makes it harder.
- **Beats (5):** the proposal, verbatim, reasonable → your body's answer
  arriving before your words (a beat about the stomach-drop, named) → the
  adjectives you're afraid of, listed by the room → what agreement would be
  *for* (peace? image? fear of loss?) → Porter: "Porter: The desk has
  processed ten thousand arrangements. The ones signed to avoid a
  conversation outlast the conversation by a week, on average."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `agree-to-keep-peace` — say yes to avoid seeming small. · Ours +6
    (surface), Grip −6, flags `consent-performed`, lucidity +6. Outcome:
    the yes buys a good week; the arrangement sits in you like swallowed
    glass; the room renders the first "exception" Jules takes — one beat,
    no details, just the notification and your face. (Flag read by
    `the-scoreboard` gate + Act III.)
  - `true-no` — say the actual no, adjectives be damned. · lucidity +20,
    Mine +6, Heart +4. Outcome: Jules's surprise, then respect; the
    conversation under the proposal finally happens (what apart is doing to
    each of you); no arrangement, one honest fight, better weather after.
  - `counter-with-need` — "here's what I actually need while we're apart."
    · lucidity +22, Open +8, Ours +4. Outcome: the negotiation that CNM
    research says distinguishes agreements that work: needs on the table
    instead of permissions; whatever you build is smaller and yours.
  - `take-it-angrily` — take the pass you didn't want, and use it to get
    even in advance. · lucidity +5, hearts −1, Grip −8, flags
    `pass-used-angry`. Outcome: written honestly from inside spite's logic;
    the encounter itself elided (threshold cut); the morning after renders
    the arithmetic — you used a stranger and a loophole to punish someone
    who hadn't done anything yet.
- **Explanation:** the researched difference between negotiated and conceded
  non-monogamy; consent that is performed vs. felt; why "I don't want this"
  is a complete sentence and rarely feels like one at 20.
- **Field note:** *Signed Under Weather* — **Conley · Moors (CNM research)**
  · body: CNM outcomes honestly — comparable satisfaction/trust *when freely
  negotiated*; agreements made from fear measure like infidelity in
  wellbeing terms; the "consent, enthusiasm, and the difference" framing.
  Margin: *a gift you can't decline is an invoice.*
- **Reflections:** yes. **Icon:** a gift box, lid ajar, dark inside.
  **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** a small wrapped box on a bed, one cold
  spotlight.

## 3. `the-rebound` — DILEMMA · you, 21

- **Psych anchor:** rebound research; the ethics of asymmetric attachment.
- **Door:** hint "The door that's still warm" · teaser "Someone kind is
  falling. You're just trying to stop the noise."
- **Premise:** three weeks after Jules ends it (or you did — dynamic beat
  honors prior flags), Mira — kind, funny, unguarded — is falling for you.
  You are using her as anesthesia and the room narrates from inside your
  numbness: her laugh lands one room away from you; the player *feels* the
  asymmetry as flattened beats on your side, vivid ones on hers.
- **Beats (5):** her toothbrush question ("should I just… leave one?") →
  your inventory of what you feel: static, gratitude, the shape where a
  feeling should be → the flashback intrusion (Jules, one beat, involuntary)
  → what Mira thinks this is, said aloud by her, wholly sincere → Porter:
  "Porter: Room service reports one guest ordering for two, and one guest
  eating alone at the same table. It happens more than you'd think."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `tell-her-what-this-is` — the anesthesia sentence, said out loud. ·
    lucidity +22, Heart +6. Outcome: the kindest cruelty on this floor;
    Mira's face; her choice then (stay knowing / go) is left with her —
    the room grants her the agency your silence was withholding.
  - `let-her-believe` — say nothing; keep warm. · lucidity +4, Mine +6,
    flags `kept-the-anesthesia`. Outcome: months pass in one beat; her
    "I love you" arrives; the room freezes on your in-breath and cuts —
    the debt now has interest (epiphany feed; Act III callback available).
  - `end-it` — end it before it costs her more. · lucidity +18, Open +4.
    Outcome: she cries and thanks you inside the same ten minutes; grief
    returns to you at full volume with nowhere to put it — the noise the
    anesthesia was masking, met at last.
  - `try-to-catch-up` — try to make yourself feel what she feels. ·
    lucidity +10, Heart +4, Grip −4. Outcome: effortful sincerity, honestly
    rendered — sometimes this works in life; the room keeps it truly
    ambiguous (50/50 dynamic beat on a doorSeed coin flip — a first for the
    engine, note for implementation) because at 21, it truly is.
- **Explanation:** rebound research (it's not uniformly bad — but asymmetric
  *information* is the injury); using people as regulation; why "I never
  promised anything" is true and insufficient.
- **Field note:** *Anesthesia With a Pulse* — **rebound literature · ethics
  of asymmetry** · body: what studies actually show about rebound
  relationships (mixed outcomes, faster recovery, real risk of harm to the
  more-invested party); informed consent as the dividing line. Margin: *the
  kind ones are load-bearing. That's why they're chosen.*
- **Reflections:** yes. **Icon:** one full cup, one empty, same saucer.
  **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** a café table, two cups, one steam plume.
