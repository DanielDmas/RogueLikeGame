# Spec 02 — Prologue + Act I: The Ground Floor (ages 15–18)

**Register: strictly non-explicit** (see charter, spec 10). Emotional and
digital dynamics only. Constellation: you, Sara (first love), Tom (best
friend), Nadia (Tom's girlfriend), the class group chat.

Conventions: effects use engine fields (`hearts` = Trust, `lucidity` =
Clarity); axes noted as Head/Heart (`reasonFeeling`), Mine/Ours
(`selfOthers`), Grip/Open (`controlAcceptance`) with sign per schema
(negative = first pole). All strings via text catalog. Every stage carries an
`explanation` ("?" = what the research says, plainly).

---

## P. `the-front-desk` — Prologue · NO-SOLUTION

- **Door:** *(entered automatically)* — teaser: "Wherever it happened, tonight is the night between."
- **Premise:** 3 a.m. A lobby that smells of carpet cleaner, cold coffee,
  and someone's perfume half-faded. The departures board lists every guest
  and no times. The Porter slides you the guest book: your name is already
  signed, in handwriting you recognize.
- **Beats (6):** waking mid-step in the lobby → the board with no times →
  the guest book, your signature, dated tomorrow → the Porter's welcome
  ("You are between one heartbeat and the next. Most guests are.") → the
  rules: floors, doors, rooms that remember → the ring on the wrong hand,
  glimpsed once, unremarked (sets up `noticed-hands` opportunities later).
- **Choices (3, orientation — mirror of `waiting-room`):**
  - `what-is-this` — "What is this place?" · lucidity +10, Head −4. Outcome:
    the Interval explained; "The rooms hold the nights people don't tell
    anyone about. You will walk them as the people inside them."
  - `whose-side` — "Whose side are you on?" · lucidity +10, Heart +4.
    Outcome: "The desk is neutral, which every guest mistakes for cruelty.
    You will be everyone before morning. Then 'side' will stop meaning much."
  - `let-me-out` — "I just want to go home." · lucidity +10, Grip −6.
    Outcome: "Home is where the conversation you are avoiding lives. The
    way back runs through every room where someone is avoiding one. No
    shortcut has ever been found. People check, nightly."
- **Explanation:** what limerence is; that the game is about experiencing
  hard relationship moments safely, from inside.
- **Field note:** *Limerence* — **Dorothy Tennov** · body: limerence as an
  involuntary state (intrusive thinking, sign-reading, the ache), distinct
  from love and from choice; the hotel is named for it because almost every
  door on these floors was opened by it. Margin: *the ones who learn to
  recognize the state no longer mistake it for instruction.*
- **Icon:** a desk bell. **Mood:** NO-SOLUTION. **Diorama (L5):** front desk
  + departures board of small emissive slats, one flickering.

## 1. `the-read-receipt` — Act I · INSIGHT · you, 15

- **Psych anchor:** attachment styles (Bowlby · Ainsworth · Hazan & Shaver).
- **Door:** hint "The door that checks its phone" · teaser "Someone is
  awake at 1 a.m., reading one word: *seen*."
- **Premise:** your bedroom, 1 a.m. Sara read your message three hours ago.
  The typing indicator has appeared and vanished four times. The room renders
  the spiral honestly: each refresh a beat, each beat a little worse.
- **Beats (5):** the phone screen in the dark → *seen, 22:04* → the typing
  dots appearing/vanishing (dynamic beat: repeats once if the player idles on
  it) → the inventory of everything you said today, re-read for crimes → the
  Porter, from nowhere: "Porter: Every guest on this floor is certain the
  silence is about them. The silence is almost never about them."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `double-text` — spiral and send two more. · Grip −8, lucidity +5.
    Outcome: relief for four minutes; then two unanswered messages instead
    of one; the arithmetic of anxiety honestly shown.
  - `drawer` — put the phone in a drawer; sit with the feeling. · Open +8,
    lucidity +20. Outcome: the feeling, met head-on, turns out to have a
    shape and a bottom; morning arrives; so does "sorry, fell asleep ❤".
  - `bait` — test her with a bait story to force a reply. · Grip −10,
    flags `first-test`, lucidity +8. Outcome: it works — she replies — and
    you learn the test worked, which is the worst thing a test can do.
    (`first-test` is read by Act I's gate and by epiphany #1.)
  - `ask-tomorrow` — ask her directly tomorrow, out loud, face to face. ·
    Head −6, lucidity +15. Outcome: daylight makes the question small; her
    answer ("my mum took the phone") makes the night's architecture visible.
- **Explanation:** attachment styles in one paragraph — anxious, avoidant,
  secure; the read-receipt spiral as the anxious loop; "the feeling is
  real, the story it tells is a draft."
- **Field note:** *The Weather You Bring* — **Bowlby · Ainsworth · Hazan &
  Shaver** · body: attachment as the childhood-learned weather system carried
  into every later room; styles are tendencies, not sentences — "earned
  security" exists and is the quiet thesis of this hotel. Margin: *the gate
  on the top floor will ask what you did with the weather.*
- **Reflections:** all four lenses on `bait` and `drawer`.
- **Icon:** a phone with one dot. **Mood:** INSIGHT. **Diorama:** a bed's
  edge + a phone glowing face-up (single cyan point light).

## 2. `the-screenshot` — Act I · DILEMMA · you, 16, the friend who knows

- **Psych anchor:** bystander disclosure; Glass's walls & windows; Darley &
  Latané (deliberate cross-title echo with ANAMNESIS's `quiet-alarm`).
- **Door:** hint "The door you didn't ask to open" · teaser "Proof arrives
  airdropped. Now it lives in your pocket."
- **Premise:** a classmate airdrops you screenshots: Tom — your best friend,
  Nadia's boyfriend — running a second, flirt-heavy chat with a girl from
  another school. Nobody else has it yet. The sender picked *you*.
- **Beats (6):** the airdrop ping in a boring lesson → reading it twice →
  what the screenshots do and don't prove (dread beat: they prove enough) →
  why you? the sender's smile across the room → the group chat humming,
  oblivious → Porter: "Porter: Evidence is never just information. It is a
  job offer. Note that you did not apply."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `tell-nadia` — show Nadia everything, today. · lucidity +18, Ours +8,
    flags `told-the-wronged`. Outcome: her face doing the thing faces do;
    the friendship-triage that follows; Tom knows within the hour who told —
    the fallout lands on you too, honestly.
  - `confront-tom` — go to Tom first: "you tell her, or I do." · lucidity
    +15, Head −5, flags `ultimatum-given`. Outcome: Tom bargains, minimizes
    ("it's just messages"), then begs; you learn an ultimatum is a promise
    you must be ready to keep — the room makes you decide whether you do.
  - `stay-out` — "not my relationship." Delete it. · lucidity +6, Mine −8,
    flags `held-the-secret`. Outcome: the secret doesn't delete; it moves in
    with you; every group hangout now has a wall in it, and you built it
    around yourself. (Read by epiphany "truths carried".)
  - `verify-first` — quietly check the screenshots are real before anything.
    · lucidity +12, Head −8. Outcome: they're real — and while you verified,
    three more people got the airdrop; thoroughness spent the only currency
    that mattered: being first, or being silent.
- **Explanation:** the "should I tell my friend their partner cheats"
  question as the everyday trolley problem: every option moves the harm;
  research on third-party disclosure and why "staying out" is also an act.
- **Field note:** *Walls, Windows, and Bystanders* — **Shirley Glass ·
  Darley & Latané** · body: Glass's architecture (intimacy = where the
  windows face; a kept secret builds a wall *for* someone else's
  relationship); the bystander logic ANAMNESIS players will recognize —
  responsibility diffuses until it belongs to no one. Margin: *another
  hotel, another wall, the same two researchers — some findings follow you
  between buildings.*
- **Reflections:** yes, on all four choices.
- **Icon:** a speech bubble with a crack. **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** a
  school chair + a floating phone rectangle, screen away from the camera.

## 3. `the-password` — Act I · DILEMMA · you, 16

- **Psych anchor:** early coercive control; reassurance-seeking loops.
- **Door:** hint "The door with two locks" · teaser "Proof of love, four to
  six digits."
- **Premise:** Sara, crying, after a bad week: her last boyfriend cheated,
  and if you loved her you'd give her your phone password. It is framed —
  sincerely, from inside her fear — as intimacy.
- **Beats (5):** the request, verbatim, with the crying → the part of you
  that wants to give it (rendered fairly — it *feels* like closeness) → the
  part that registers a lock turning → her: "what do you have to hide?" →
  Porter: "Porter: A key given under a deadline opens a different door than
  a key given freely. Same metal. Different room."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `give-it` — give her the password. · Ours +8, Grip −6, lucidity +5.
    Outcome: *the room rewards it short-term, honestly* — she softens, the
    week heals; and a month later you catch yourself pre-editing texts to
    friends; the surveillance is in your thumbs now. Flags `gave-the-key`.
  - `refuse-flat` — no. Just no. · Mine +8, lucidity +12. Outcome: the fight
    that follows is real and costs a beat of the relationship; the room does
    not pretend boundaries are free — only that they're load-bearing.
  - `transparency-not-surveillance` — "ask me anything, any time. The lock
    stays." · lucidity +22, Open +8. Outcome: the distinction lands slowly;
    the field note carries the science; the best available door, and still
    not painless — trust rebuilt by hand, not by keys.
  - `demand-hers` — "fine — yours too, then." · Grip −10, flags
    `symmetry-trap`, lucidity +8. Outcome: mutually assured surveillance;
    two people reading each other's mail in adjacent rooms; the room lets
    the silence of that arrangement speak.
- **Explanation:** why "prove it" requests escalate (reassurance relieves
  anxiety briefly and feeds it long-term); the difference between
  transparency (offered) and surveillance (extracted); early-warning signs
  of control framed as love.
- **Field note:** *The Key Under Duress* — **coercive-control research ·
  reassurance-seeking studies** · body: monitoring soothes the monitor for
  hours and corrodes both people for months; control's early costume is
  always care; the reliable tell is what happens when you say no. Margin:
  *locks are honest. Deadlines aren't.*
- **Reflections:** yes.
- **Icon:** a padlock with a heart keyhole. **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:**
  two phones on one bed, both face-down, one point light between.

## 4. `the-party` — Act I · DILEMMA · you, 17

- **Psych anchor:** moral disengagement (Bandura); alcohol myopia (Steele &
  Josephs).
- **Door:** hint "The door with music behind it" · teaser "Somebody's
  parents are away. Somebody's girlfriend is too."
- **Premise:** Sara is visiting her grandmother. The party plays a kissing
  dare; the bottle lands on you and on Klara from the parallel class. The
  room's craft: render each rationalization *forming in real time* — "it's a
  game," "she'd laugh," "everyone's watching," "it doesn't count" — as beats
  the player reads while deciding. Non-explicit throughout: a dare, a kiss,
  a room of phones.
- **Beats (6):** the circle, the bottle, the spin in real time → the landing
  → the rationalizations arriving one per beat, each perfectly reasonable →
  the room of raised phones (everything here is recorded, a Ground Floor
  constant) → Klara's shrug, kind and unbothered → Porter: "Porter: 'It
  just happened' is assembled in advance, piece by piece. You are watching
  the assembly line now."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `play-and-bury` — play the dare; tell no one. · lucidity +5, flags
    `it-didnt-count`. Outcome: the kiss is nothing; the burying is
    something; the phones were up — the room closes on a screen lighting in
    someone's pocket, unsent. (Flag read by Act I gate + Act II callbacks.)
  - `play-and-tell` — play it; tell Sara yourself, tonight. · lucidity +18,
    Heart +6. Outcome: her reaction is hers — hurt, then oddly settled by
    having been told *first*; the telling costs less than the finding-out
    would, and the room shows the difference arithmetic.
  - `refuse` — refuse the dare; absorb the mockery. · lucidity +15, Mine +4,
    flags `refused-the-dare` (**keepsake earn: `the-cheap-ring`**). Outcome:
    thirty seconds of jeering that feels like an hour; then the party moves
    on, because parties do; someone you didn't notice noticed.
  - `leave` — just leave the party. · lucidity +10, Open +4. Outcome: the
    cold air outside as a full beat; nothing happened, and you learn that
    "nothing happened" can be a thing you *did* rather than a thing you
    avoided.
- **Explanation:** Bandura's moral disengagement in teen language — how the
  brain pre-writes the excuse before the act; alcohol myopia (what's in
  front of you gets louder, consequences get quieter); why "it was a game"
  works on everyone except the person it happened to.
- **Field note:** *The Assembly of "It Just Happened"* — **Bandura · Steele
  & Josephs** · body: disengagement mechanisms by name (euphemism, diffusion,
  advantageous comparison); myopia as the dimmer switch on the future.
  Margin: *no one at this hotel ever meant to check in.*
- **Reflections:** yes.
- **Icon:** a bottle mid-spin. **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** a ring of
  small chair silhouettes, one bottle-shaped glint at center.

## 5. `the-forward` — Act I · DILEMMA · you, 16, bystander

- **Psych anchor:** consent & digital permanence; bystander action;
  the legal reality of non-consensual image sharing.
- **Content rule (absolute):** nothing is ever depicted or described. The
  diorama's phone faces away. The beats never characterize the image beyond
  "a photo that was never meant for this chat." The entire room is the
  ethics of what your thumb does next.
- **Door:** hint "The door that is already open" · teaser "It arrived in the
  group chat forty seconds ago. Forty people."
- **Premise:** an intimate photo of a classmate, Ema, hits the class group
  chat, forwarded by someone she trusted. The chat is doing what chats do:
  jokes, screenshots-of-screenshots, three people typing at once.
- **Beats (5):** the ping; the realization; your thumb hovering → the chat's
  laughter scrolling in real time (dread rendered as message beats) → Ema is
  in your maths class; she doesn't know yet → what forwarding is, said
  plainly by the room, once → Porter: "Porter: Everyone in that chat is
  telling themselves they are only an audience. Audiences are how this
  works."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `delete-only` — delete it and say nothing. · lucidity +8, Mine −4.
    Outcome: your hands are clean and nothing is; the chat scrolls on; the
    room quietly counts the other thirty-nine audiences.
  - `report` — report it — to the platform and to an adult at school. ·
    lucidity +20, Head −6, flags `reported-it`. Outcome: adults move slower
    than chats and faster than nothing; consequences arrive for the sender;
    the chat calls you a name for a week. The room lets that cost be real.
  - `tell-ema-first` — message Ema first, so she isn't the last to know. ·
    lucidity +22, Heart +8, Ours +6, flags `told-her-first`. Outcome: the
    hardest message you've ever typed; her reply is two words; years later
    (one flash-forward beat, a Ground Floor first) she remembers who told
    her — the room's rare unambiguous kindness, still not painless.
  - `confront-publicly` — call the sender out in the chat itself. · lucidity
    +12, Grip −4. Outcome: the chat turns on him, then on the drama, then —
    the room is honest — partly back on Ema; public confrontation as a blunt
    instrument that hits several people.
- **Explanation:** consent doesn't transfer with a file; forwarding is a
  *new* act, not a continuation; in most jurisdictions sharing intimate
  images of minors is a serious crime for every forwarder, full stop; what
  actually helps the person in the photo (being told, being believed, adults
  who act).
- **Field note:** *The Fortieth Audience* — **non-consensual image-sharing
  research · the legal reality** · body: the one field note allowed to be
  blunt about law; harm research (the injury is the audience, and it
  compounds per forward); bystander-to-upstander findings. Margin: *the
  original sender pressed one button. So did everyone after.*
- **Reflections:** yes (duty lens is central here).
- **Icon:** a paper plane, broken. **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** a phone
  face-down, screen-light leaking from under it like something spilled.

## 6. `the-best-friend's-girl` — Act I · NO-SOLUTION · you, 17

- **Psych anchor:** limerence (Tennov); mate poaching (Schmitt & Buss).
- **Door:** hint "The door you keep walking past" · teaser "She laughs at
  your jokes a half-second too long. You've started counting."
- **Premise:** Nadia. Tom's Nadia. The room is pure limerence anatomy —
  and its craft is structural: **intrusive-thought beats interrupt other
  beats** (dynamic beats that splice mid-paragraph), so the player
  experiences intrusion rather than reading about it.
- **Beats (6, with 2 intrusions):** an ordinary study evening at Tom's →
  *[intrusion: the half-second laugh, replayed]* → the loyal inventory of
  everything Tom is to you → *[intrusion: the bus stop last Tuesday, her
  sleeve]* → the arithmetic of signs (limerence's sign-reading rendered
  honestly, and its unreliability noted by the room itself) → Porter:
  "Porter: The state you are in has a clinical name, a documented arc, and
  no steering wheel. Conduct, however, still has one."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `confess-to-her` — tell Nadia. · lucidity +10, Heart +8, flags
    `crossed-the-line`. Outcome: whatever she feels (the room keeps it
    ambiguous — a half-second is not a contract), the constellation now has
    a crack through it; Tom's next "you okay?" lands like a bell.
  - `starve-it` — distance, no drama, no explanation. · lucidity +18, Open
    +6. Outcome: limerence starved is limerence loud, for a while — the
    intrusion beats *increase* for one more room (flag-driven), then fade;
    the honest cost of the honest option.
  - `tell-tom` — tell Tom the truth of the feeling. · lucidity +22, Mine −4.
    Outcome: the bravest sentence on this floor; Tom's silence, then: "okay.
    thanks for telling me and not her." The friendship survives, changed —
    a wall replaced by a window, at the price of comfort.
  - `test-the-evening` — engineer one ambiguous evening to find out. ·
    lucidity +8, Grip −8, flags `ran-the-test`. Outcome: ambiguity engineered
    returns ambiguity, compounded; now there are two people counting
    half-seconds; the room declines to resolve which.
- **Explanation:** limerence as involuntary state vs. action as choice; why
  sign-reading feels like evidence and isn't; the difference between a
  feeling you must own and a move you may make.
- **Field note:** *The State With No Steering Wheel* — **Tennov · Schmitt &
  Buss** · body: limerence's documented arc (onset, crystallization, decay
  6–24 months untended); mate-poaching research and its costs to all three
  corners; feelings as weather, conduct as navigation. Margin: *every guest
  who ever burned a friendship down swore the laugh was a signal.*
- **Reflections:** yes.
- **Icon:** two overlapping circles, one dashed. **Mood:** NO-SOLUTION.
  **Diorama:** two chairs close together and one apart, a warm lamp on the
  far one.

## 7. `the-summer-ends` — Act I · DILEMMA · you, 18

- **Psych anchor:** Rusbult's investment model; honest endings.
- **Door:** hint "The door with a suitcase beside it" · teaser "Two cities,
  one calendar, and everyone has an opinion."
- **Premise:** university in another city starts in three weeks. You and
  Sara (if prior flags allow, dynamic beat acknowledges the year's history —
  `gave-the-key`, `it-didnt-count`, etc.) must decide what September means.
- **Beats (5):** the packing, itemized (Chattam precision: what goes in the
  box is the relationship's museum) → everyone's opinions, quoted → the
  actual question under the logistics → what you each said you wanted, once,
  at the start → Porter: "Porter: Guests decide this one in the corridor,
  mostly. The room only makes them say it out loud."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `promise-ldr` — promise the long-distance everyone says will fail. ·
    lucidity +12, Heart +8, Ours +6, flags `promised-september`. Outcome:
    the promise is real and so are base rates; the room refuses to spoil
    which you'll be; Act II's `the-distance` reads this flag.
  - `end-clean` — end it now, on the best day rather than the worst. ·
    lucidity +20, Head −8. Outcome: the strange dignity of a chosen ending;
    grief without a villain; the room notes that "on time" is a place
    almost nobody manages to leave from.
  - `drift` — promise nothing; let it fade. · lucidity +4, Grip −4, Mine +4,
    flags `let-it-drift`. Outcome: the coward's route honestly costed — no
    scene, no ending, and eighteen months later no word for what you were;
    the unfinished sentence follows you (epiphany feed).
  - `open-until-christmas` — propose an arrangement neither of you
    understands yet. · lucidity +10, Open +6, flags `first-open`. Outcome:
    the word "open" doing more work than either of you can lift at 18; terms
    undefined, revisited painfully in Act II (`the-hall-pass` callback).
- **Explanation:** the investment model in one breath — commitment ≈
  satisfaction + investment − alternatives — and why relationships continue
  or end for reasons that have nothing to do with love's presence.
- **Field note:** *The Arithmetic of Staying* — **Caryl Rusbult** · body:
  the model, its evidence base, its uncomfortable predictive power; sunk
  cost's first appearance (harvested fully in Act III's `the-wedding-eve`).
  Margin: *the suitcase was packed either way. Only the label changed.*
- **Reflections:** yes.
- **Icon:** a suitcase with a heart-shaped sticker, corner peeling.
  **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** a suitcase silhouette, lid open, warm
  light inside.

## G. `the-rumor` — Act I GATE · DOOMED · you, 17

- **Psych anchor:** confirmation bias (Nickerson); the Othello test.
- **Door:** hint "The door everyone else already opened" · teaser "The whole
  school knows something. Nobody knows what."
- **Premise:** the school says Sara cheated at the lake weekend. The
  evidence: one blurry story, two friends-of-friends, and her odd quietness
  on Sunday. The room is DOOMED and says so: every path costs something;
  the only variable is *what*.
- **Beats (6):** the rumor arriving thirdhand → the evidence, itemized, and
  its holes, itemized (the room forces both lists) → what the not-knowing is
  doing to you, hour by hour → her "what's wrong?" text, sitting unanswered
  → the three shapes of what you could do next → Porter: "Porter: There are
  tests that measure a thing, and tests that create it. The desk has stopped
  stocking the first kind. Guests never asked for them."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `trust-without-asking` — extend the Benefit of the Doubt whole; never
    ask. · Trust preserved, lucidity +15, Open +10. Outcome: you live with
    never knowing — permanently (the room confirms: no later beat will
    resolve it, and none does); trust as a decision rather than a
    conclusion; heavier than it sounds, and the room weighs it honestly.
  - `interrogate` — ask her everything, dates and names. · lucidity +18,
    hearts −1, Grip −8. Outcome: answers arrive and so does the damage —
    the questions themselves inform her what you think she is; whatever the
    truth was, the relationship now contains the interrogation forever.
  - `set-the-trap` — tell her a fake detail only a guilty person would
    correct. · lucidity +20, **loseMemory: true** (the Benefit of the Doubt,
    burned), flags `set-the-trap`. Outcome: the trap works — the room lets
    it *work* — and the knowledge arrives in the same envelope as this:
    you are now a person who traps people you love. Both facts are
    permanent. The final gate reads this back verbatim.
  - `ask-the-accuser` — go to the source: "what do you get out of this?" ·
    lucidity +25, Head −6. Outcome: the rare third door — the rumor's
    engine exposed (a grudge, a bored weekend, a misread photo); not
    vindication, but the discovery that the question "is it true?" had an
    older sibling: "who benefits?"
- **Explanation:** confirmation bias in plain terms; why suspicion generates
  its own evidence; the Othello dynamic (the test creates the guilt it
  sought — in one of you or the other); what "benefit of the doubt"
  actually costs and buys.
- **Field note:** *The Test That Creates Its Result* — **Nickerson ·
  Othello** · body: confirmation bias's mechanics; surveillance and trap-
  setting as self-fulfilling instruments; the asymmetry that certainty about
  a partner is purchasable only in currencies that debase the relationship.
  Margin: *of the guests who set the trap, every single one caught
  something.*
- **Reflections:** yes, all four choices — this gate is the Examined Path's
  Act I centerpiece.
- **Icon:** a magnifying glass with a crack. **Mood:** DOOMED. **Diorama:**
  a corridor of small identical doors, one ajar, cold light.
