  corridor's carpet hush → the shorthand's greatest hits, involuntarily

  one full beat, nothing in it but breathing → Porter (from the service

    Outcome: the anticlimax honored as the achievement it is; your room's

    Outcome: the naming changes the shorthand forever — some of it dies,
