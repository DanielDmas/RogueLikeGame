# Spec 05 — Act IV: The Top Floor (31–35) + The Records Office (understory)

**Register: frank/mature, never graphic** (charter, spec 10). Act IV is a
fixed corridor of 3 (the `ACT4_SEQUENCE` analog). Constellation: one
household — you, the spouse, two kids asleep upstairs, a phone face-down on
the kitchen table. The Records Office mirrors ANAMNESIS's understory:
offered as a second, stranger door beside `the-kitchen-table` for returning
guests only (`prior.runs >= 1`), rejoining the corridor after.

---

## 1. `the-kitchen-table` — DILEMMA · you, 33

- **Psych anchor:** Amato (children fare by conflict, not category); Perel
  (exit vs. alarm).
- **Door:** hint "The door to the quietest room in the house" · teaser
  "Everything is known now. The coffee is going cold either way."
- **Premise:** the morning after full disclosure. **The room adapts via
  flags to *which* everything** (dynamic beats, the engine's core trick):
  yours (`confessed-whole`/`crossed-at-the-conference`/`trickle-truth`
  lineage), theirs (`the-discovery` lineage), or both. Kids asleep
  upstairs — their weight in the house rendered as acoustics: every beat
  notes what volume it can afford. Two chairs, one table, the phone now
  face-up and irrelevant.
- **Beats (6, flag-adaptive):** the kitchen at 06:40, itemized (Chattam
  domestic: the drawing on the fridge is load-bearing) → the night behind
  you, summarized in whichever shape the flags dictate → the first
  sentence of the rest of it — who says it (dynamic) → the four futures,
  seated at the table like guests → what the children change and what
  they don't (the room is surgical here: they are a reason for *quality*,
  not automatically for *staying*) → Porter (in the hallway mirror only):
  "Porter: Top-floor mornings are the only part of the hotel I cannot
  enter. The desk merely confirms: the table is load-bearing. Build on
  it or clear it. It will hold either."
- **Choices (4):**
  - `stay-for-them` — stay for the kids; say so out loud, to each other.
    · lucidity +12, Ours +6, flags `stayed-for-kids`. Outcome: the pact
    rendered honestly — a decision that can be noble scaffolding or a
    twenty-year deferral, and the room shows the fork *inside* the
    choice: named and revisited, it can hold; named and shelved, it
    becomes `the-drift` with children in it (dynamic callback if
    `already-gone` is set).
  - `separate-well` — separate, and spend everything on doing it well. ·
    lucidity +20, hearts −1, Open +6. Outcome: the un-dramatic
    catastrophe: logistics as elegy (one beat is just the calendar of
    handovers); Amato's finding embodied — the conflict, not the
    category, is what the kids will carry; grief done in daylight.
  - `attempt-repair` — the work. Not the word — the work. · lucidity
    +25, Heart +6, Ours +8, flags `chose-repair`. Outcome: no montage,
    no guarantee — the room renders week one only: the therapist's
    intake form, the first honest inventory, the affair (whosever it
    was) examined as alarm rather than only as crime (Perel, carefully);
    the final beat is the second appointment kept, which is the whole
    victory available by morning.
  - `say-the-unsayable` — say the one thing you've each been holding
    since before any of this. · lucidity +30, hearts −1. Outcome: the
    room's riskiest door — two sentences, one each (flag-harvested where
    possible: "I was lonely years before anyone touched anyone" / "I
    knew, and I chose not to know") — after which every other choice on
    this table changes meaning; the room ends *without resolving*,
    because the sentence was the event.
- **Explanation:** what research actually says about children and
  separation (conflict exposure is the active ingredient; well-managed
  separation beats high-conflict staying); the affair-as-alarm reframe
  and its limits (alarm explains; it does not excuse); why "for the
  kids" needs a maintenance schedule.
- **Field note:** *The Load-Bearing Table* — **Amato · Perel** · body:
  the children-and-conflict literature honestly (effect sizes, the
  well-being curves); Perel's exit/alarm distinction with its critics
  noted; repair's actual predictors (structure, witness, time). Margin:
  *the drawing on the fridge survives every version of this morning.
  Decide which morning it grows up inside.*
- **Reflections:** yes — all four lenses at full depth; this room is the
  Examined Path's summit. **Icon:** a table with two cups. **Mood:**
  DILEMMA. **Diorama:** kitchen table under one hanging bulb, two chairs
  angled, a child's drawing rectangle glowing faint on the fridge.

## 2. `the-unsent` — DILEMMA · you, 34 — the `last-message` analog

- **Psych anchor:** disclosure, closure, and letters-never-sent
  (expressive-writing research).
- **Door:** hint "The door with a letter slot" · teaser "One message may
  leave the hotel tonight. The desk guarantees delivery. Nothing else."
- **Premise:** a writing desk at the corridor's end, one envelope, the
  Porter with the postal cap's analog (a brass letter-scale). One
  message leaves tonight — it will arrive as a dream, an impulse, a
  song on a radio. Availability conditions mirror `last-message`
  exactly (axes/flags gate the options).
- **Choices (6, availability-gated):**
  - `to-the-one-you-hurt` — available if any betrayal flag set. ·
    lucidity +20, Ours +8. Outcome: the amends letter, written without
    requesting absolution (the room strikes the sentence that asks —
    literally, a beat where the scale rejects the draft until the ask
    is removed).
  - `to-the-one-who-hurt-you` — available on betrayed-lineage flags. ·
    lucidity +18, Open +6. Outcome: not forgiveness — *release*; the
    letter that ends the sentence "you still owe me" by tearing up the
    invoice, for the writer's sake.
  - `to-the-one-that-got-away` — available if `walked-away` or
    `end-clean` lineage. · lucidity +15, Heart +6. Outcome: the letter
    honest people are afraid of; the room permits it exactly one
    sentence of warmth beyond closure, then weighs it, then sends it.
  - `to-your-16-year-old-self` — always available; the room's kindest
    door. · lucidity +25. Outcome: down the letter slot and four floors
    to the Ground Floor; a dynamic beat quotes one of your actual Act I
    choices back with tenderness; the sentence every adult in this
    hotel needed at 16, written by the only person qualified.
  - `to-your-own-kids-someday` — available if `the-kitchen-table`
    played. · lucidity +20, Ours +6. Outcome: sealed, dated, "open
    when you're old enough to be in rooms like these"; the education

  - `blank-page` — send the blank page. · lucidity +12, Open +4.
    Outcome: the heaviest envelope (the `silence` analog kept nearly
    verbatim in spirit): everything unsaid still weighs something;
    delivered as a doorway pause, a felt accompaniment, four seconds.
- **Explanation:** expressive-writing research (Pennebaker lineage,
  soft-cited): why writing the unsent letter measurably helps the
  writer; closure as something manufactured, not found.
- **Field note:** composed by the room from the player's own choice —
  the codex entry is the message sent (the `last-message` self-writing
  note, verbatim mechanic).
- **Reflections:** yes. **Icon:** an envelope on a scale. **Mood:**
  DILEMMA. **Diorama:** a writing desk, one lit envelope on a brass
  scale.

## 3. `the-morning-desk` — GATE · INSIGHT · you, 35 — the `door-that-asks` analog

- **Psych anchor:** narrative identity (the audited self); the whole
  game's read-back.
- **Door:** hint "The desk where you check out" · teaser "The Porter has
  your file open. It's thicker than you remember writing."
- **Premise:** checkout. The Porter reads your file back — **dynamic
  beats verbatim from the run's flags**, mirroring `door-that-asks`'s
  structure exactly: the screenshot and what you did with it; the party;
  the confession made, carried, or trickled; the trap if you set it (the
  burned Benefit of the Doubt read back like the burned photograph:
  "There is a hole in your file where the not-knowing used to live. I
  can see it from here. Was it worth it?").
- **Stage 1 — the audit (3 choices):** `stand-by-all` (steadiness or
  armor — "from this side of the desk they are identical") / `name-what-
  changed-me` (revision with receipts — the answer the desk is *for*;
  the room's lock-becomes-hinge beat) / `some-rooms-i-wasnt-present-in`
  (the honest gap; the desk's response branches on whether the file
  shows a real hole (`memoryLost` analog) or convenient fog — verbatim
  `dont-remember` mechanic).
- **Stage 2 — the threshold (5 doors):**

  - `stop-carrying-it` — lie down; the Interval finishes. · lucidity

  - `[hidden] the-laughing-door` — available: `noticed-hands` flags ≥2

    small door with laughter behind it → ending `the-mirror`.
  - `[hidden] i-know-every-room` — available: `the-pattern` eligibility
    (anamnesis analog: full codex + 2 keepsake-choices + Clarity
    threshold + `memoryLost`-analog false + no `chose-not-to-know`).

# The Records Office — understory (basement; returning guests; sequence of 3)

Offering mechanics identical to ANAMNESIS's understory: a second,
violet-accented door beside `the-kitchen-table` for `prior.runs >= 1`,

## U1. `the-registry` — NO-SOLUTION — the archive analog

- **Door:** hint "The stairs behind the desk" · teaser "Your last stay
  is already filed. Filing is all the basement does."
- **Premise & mechanics:** shelf of guest files; yours, newest, stamped
  COMPLETE. FILED. NOT FORGOTTEN. One card quotes one prior-run choice
  verbatim (`pickExhibitEntry`); the intake form carries your persona
  blurb (`{blurb}` token, same as `the-archive`).
- **Choices (4):** `sign-it` (own it — "you added your name to a night
  that already happened; that costs something; it isn't nothing") /
  `disown-it` ("the file does not argue. It only keeps.") /
  `refile-unjudged` (close the lid mid-sentence, respectfully) /
  `pin-the-keycard` (keepsake amendment — ✧, requires `the-keycard`
  held).
- **Field note:** *On Keeping Files* — **narrative identity (Ricœur
  echo, cross-title)** · margin: *you are not obliged to endorse every
  night in the file. Only to admit whose handwriting it is.*
- **Icon:** a file box. **Mood:** NO-SOLUTION. **Diorama:** basement
  shelving, one box pulled out, one desk lamp.

## U2. `the-doors-not-opened` — DILEMMA — the unchosen analog

- **Door:** hint "The corridor kept at temperature" · teaser "Every
  door you walked past, still ajar. One is opening."
- **Premise & mechanics:** the corridor of last run's unvisited rooms
  (`pickUnchosenRooms`): titles listed dynamically; one swings open.
  **The honest beat this pack adds:** the affair you didn't have is
  sometimes a door you were never offered — unopened is not
  automatically virtue, and the room says so once, plainly.
- **Choices (3):** `enter-late` (curiosity honored late — "smaller than
  you built it. Most unlived things are.") / `close-it` (respect its
  pastness) / `ask-why-now` (the oiled-hinges beat: "the basement
  offers the door you are now ready to survive not opening").
- **Field note:** *The Road Not Taken, Audited* — **Kierkegaard's
  vertigo of the possible · Frost misread, corrected** (cross-title
  echo, re-aimed at relationships: the person you didn't choose is a
  rumor, not a receipt).
- **Icon:** a door ajar. **Mood:** DILEMMA. **Diorama:** door
  corridor, one warm slit widening.

## U3. `the-other-side` — INSIGHT — the echo analog, **sharpened into the thesis**

- **Door:** hint "The room with the second chair" · teaser "Someone in
  there talks exactly like you. That's the problem."
- **Premise & mechanics:** two chairs. The voice in the other chair is
  assembled from **your own recorded lines** (`pickShadowMoments` +
  transcript quotes) — *replayed as the person across from you heard
  them.* Same skeleton as `the-echo` (junction-analog beat reads your
  gate choices; ending-title beat reads `prior.endingId`), plus the
  pack's one addition: a register shift — your sentences played back
  a half-tone flatter, the way defensiveness sounds from the receiving
  chair.
- **Choices (3):** `answer-yourself` ("I hear you" — acknowledgment,
  not correction) / `let-yourself-finish` (the uninterrupted hearing
  you may never have given anyone) / `sit-in-both-chairs` (the
  costliest reading: there was never a guest here — only a series of
  you, taking turns holding the sentence).
- **Explanation:** perspective-taking as the one reliably protective
  relationship skill — the whole hotel's syllabus in one room; why
  hearing yourself from the other chair changes conduct when no
  argument does.
- **Field note:** *The Second Chair* — **perspective-taking research**
  · body: the findings (perspective-taking interventions measurably
  reduce destructive conflict; the effect survives even brief
  exercises); the honest limit (it is a skill, it decays, it must be
  practiced — this room is the practice). Margin: *every fight in
  this hotel had two first-person narrators. The basement keeps both
  recordings.*
- **Icon:** two chairs facing. **Mood:** INSIGHT. **Diorama:** two
  chairs close, one lamp between, one chair's seat slightly warm-lit.
