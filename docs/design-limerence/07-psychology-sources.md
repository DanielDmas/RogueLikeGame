# Spec 07 — Psychology Sources & Citation-Honesty Rules

## 1. Why this doc exists

LIMERENCE's credibility *is* its field notes. ANAMNESIS's R10 audit
established the standard: **no claim in a field note may overstate its
source.** This doc lists the anchor set per theme and the binding rules
for citing each. Where a finding is contested, the note carries the
contest. Where a number is soft, the note says "surveys suggest." Where a
concept is folklore, it appears only *labeled* as folklore.

## 2. Binding citation rules (inherited from R10, extended)

1. **Contested headline claims carry their critique in the same note.**
   Flagship case: Gottman's "94% divorce prediction" — the note must
   include the published critique (post-hoc fitting; prediction vs.
   postdiction). The Four Horsemen and repair-attempt findings stand on
   firmer ground and may be stated plainly.
2. **Soft numbers stay soft.** Infidelity prevalence, disclosure
   preferences, CNM population figures: "surveys suggest," never hard
   percentages.
3. **Folklore is labeled.** Love languages (weak evidence, commercial
   origin), "the 7-year itch," Mars/Venus — excluded, or explicitly
   framed as folklore if a room's texture wants them.
4. **Both directions on genuinely open questions.** The
   confess-vs-carry debate, exit-vs-alarm, veto ethics: steel-man both;
   the game refuses verdicts and says so in as many words.
5. **CNM is treated with the same evidentiary respect as monogamy.**
   Neither romanticized nor pathologized: negotiated CNM shows
   comparable satisfaction/trust in the Conley/Moors lineage; its
   failure modes are specific and named.
6. **Law is cited only where it is bright-line** (one place:
   `the-forward`'s non-consensual imagery note), phrased
   jurisdiction-generically ("in most jurisdictions… a serious crime").
7. **Nothing clinical is diagnosed at the player.** Epiphanies observe
   behavior in-fiction; field notes describe research populations.
   "This is fiction, not therapy" (charter) is load-bearing.

## 4. Voice rules for notes

Field-note voice carries over from ANAMNESIS: a knowledgeable, faintly
wry archivist; one **bolded thesis sentence** per note; one margin line
in an older hand. LIMERENCE's margin voice is the hotel's ledger — dry,
factual, devastating (*"of the guests who set the trap, every single one
caught something"*). Notes never address the player's own choice — they
describe the terrain, not the traveler (charter: observation, never
verdict).
